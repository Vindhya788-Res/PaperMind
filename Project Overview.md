# Project Overview

# PaperMind

**Version:** 1.0

**Project Type:** AI Research Platform

**Domain:** Natural Language Processing (NLP), Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Scientific Literature Understanding

---

# 1. Introduction

PaperMind is an AI-assisted scientific literature understanding platform designed to help researchers efficiently explore, analyze, compare, and synthesize information from large collections of research papers.

The project is not intended to become another generic PDF chatbot. Instead, it is designed as a research platform that enables experimentation with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) techniques, document retrieval strategies, evidence synthesis, and scientific knowledge discovery.

The software itself is only one outcome of the project.

The primary objective is to create a platform that supports publishable research while simultaneously serving as a strong software engineering portfolio.

---

# 2. Background

The rapid increase in scientific publications makes it increasingly difficult for researchers to remain up to date.

Researchers often spend weeks reading papers before beginning a literature review.

Current tools provide assistance in searching and summarizing documents but still have several limitations:

- Limited transparency in retrieval
- Weak comparison across multiple papers
- Poor identification of conflicting findings
- Limited support for evidence synthesis
- Lack of research gap identification
- Minimal evaluation of retrieval quality

These limitations provide opportunities for further research.
